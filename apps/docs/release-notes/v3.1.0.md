# CapuBridge v3.1.0

Phones connect over Wi-Fi by scanning a QR code, the command palette reaches every screen and
action, and Settings is rebuilt on Cladd. A frozen app no longer makes the context bar flicker.

## Connect over Wi-Fi

- **QR first.** The Connect over Wi-Fi panel opens on a large QR code with three numbered steps
  and live states: waiting, pairing, connecting, connected. Failures are said in plain words
  with one Retry.
- **Pairing code** is the second method. The address is filled in from the phone's pairing
  service, so only the six digits are typed, and the phone connects on its own afterwards.
- **Cable or address** keeps Switch to Wi-Fi for Android 10 and older, and connect by address.
- **Nearby phones** lists every phone advertising wireless debugging, with one-click Connect.
- **Remembered phones** reconnect by themselves when they are nearby, if you leave Reconnect by
  itself on. Each one is retried at most every 30 seconds, and only while the window is visible.
- Pairing runs in the Rust backend. The pairing password stays inside the pairing session and is
  never logged.

### Fixed

- **Disconnect** now works for phones connected through mDNS, and a disconnected phone stays
  disconnected until you connect it again or restart the app.

## Command palette

- **Sections**: Recent, Go to, Apps, Devices and targets, Actions and Tables.
- **Prefixes** with a mode chip:
  - `open` lists the device's installed apps with their icons and launches one;
  - `>` narrows to actions;
  - `@` to devices and targets;
  - `#` to tables and stores;
  - `:` to this screen.

  Backspace on an empty input leaves the mode.

- **Every screen is an entry** (Data › SQLite, Device › Files, Settings › Appearance…). Each
  entry says quietly what it still needs, such as a device or a WebView.
- **`#` jumps to a table or store** of the database open in the SQLite or IndexedDB tab.
- **Actions from anywhere** on the selected app:
  - restart, force stop and clear data, which ask first, with clear data in red;
  - screenshot and screen recording while the device streams;
  - record or stop a session;
  - copy the device serial or the WebView address;
  - reload the WebView.
- Fuzzy matching takes the words of the query in any order. The list is virtualised. Recents are
  kept, up to 8.

## Settings

- **A two-pane dialog**, like the Device manager: sections on the left and each page in Cladd
  cards. The pages are Appearance, Devices, Screen, Shortcuts, AI access and Updates.
- **Devices** lists the phones paired over Wi-Fi. You can turn Reconnect by itself on or off
  for each one, or forget it.
- **Screen** holds the mirror's frame rate, the recording quality and the bitrate.
- **Appearance** gains a switch to show or hide the navigation rail.
- **Shortcuts** lists the console drawer's keys instead of the old dock's.
- **AI access** is split into small cards: server, connection, clients, config and tools.
- Links to the old General, ADB, Chrome and Theme pages still work.
- **Light themes are not available yet.** Their cards show "Coming later". If a light theme was
  saved, the app opens in the dark one instead.

## Fixed

- **No more flicker on a frozen app.** With the app in the background on the phone, the context
  bar used to switch between "attaching" and "in the background" about once a second. Now
  CapuBridge waits up to 8 seconds for the app to answer before it opens the connection.
  Network capture also waits longer between reconnect attempts, from half a second up to 8
  seconds.
