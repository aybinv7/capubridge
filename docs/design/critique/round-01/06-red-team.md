## 0. Verdict in six lines

1. **The panel is mostly right, and the product is weaker than the owner's 7.** Of 41 S0/S1 findings
   I re-opened, 31 are confirmed, 6 are overstated, 2 are understated and 2 are wrong or
   unverified on the evidence. My corrected overall is **4.7**, against a team self-score of about 6.9.
2. **The honesty defects are real and they are the headline.** IndexedDB claims "none found" while
   paused, Network shows a green `live` under a `paused` context, Performance draws zeros and
   "Discharging" next to a charging phone, and the agent is bound to a different app than the context.
   I confirmed all four, with screenshot and `file:line`.
3. **Two S0s do not survive.** "Mirror streaming while black" is wrong on the evidence: the mirrored
   status bar renders in every cited frame, so frames are arriving. "Two Android users mixed" is
   overstated: only the App page's path is wrong; the SQLite reads hit the right user on this device.
4. **The biggest contradiction is the companion app.** Reports 01, 03 and 04 promise SDK-free field
   capture of the app's _data_. Report 02 says that is impossible, and I verified it in the Chromium
   source: the WebView DevTools socket admits only root, shell or the app's own UID. The owner must
   decide on the in-app SDK. It is the decision every other plan depends on.
5. **Everyone missed three things.** The website overclaims more than report 01 found (benchmarks,
   scenario diffs, "lines up with the interaction"). The SQLite surface has no background banner and
   its only freshness chip shows _query time_, which reads as data age. App-private database copies
   can be left on the device and are kept in the host temp folder.
6. **The evidence pack is not good enough for round 2.** `panel-01` is not Device, `panel-12` is not
   the palette, and no screen shows the app live with data, a loaded Replay, the dock, first run, or a
   missing or unauthorised device.

## 6. What the owner must decide

1. **The in-app debug SDK (T3): yes or no.** This is the decision every other plan depends on (X1, §2).
   Without it, the companion delivers presence, consent, screen capture, device vitals and VPN-level
   network metadata, but not the app's data, console or DOM in the field. With it, the product
   stops being SDK-free, and the website and positioning must say so.
2. **The category word and the rename window.** Pick one of "recorder", "field debugger" or "evidence
   workbench" (X2), staged honestly against what ships. Rename **before** the companion's first Play
   listing, because an application ID is permanent. Trademark and domain clearance go to Direction.
3. **Brand architecture.** One evidence format and one on-device recorder across Capubridge, Capuchoo and
   `@cavulsqa/recorder`, or an explicit boundary (debug and field against production fleets).
4. **Companion distribution.** ADB-pushed only, a Play "Lite" listing, or both, and whether VPN capture
   ships at all, given that Proxyman's standalone Android app already occupies that slot (§2).
5. **The aya dex licence (AGPL origin in an MIT project).** This is legal: escalate to the Tech Lead or
   Direction before the next public release.
6. **Customer-specific code in the core** (`automation.types.ts:137-226`, `acceptance.ts`). Move it to a
   plugin or fixture, or accept that the generic product carries one client's scenario.
7. **An honesty freeze.** Make "one derived state, one Gate, provenance with read time" a release blocker
   ahead of any new feature. Recommended: yes.
8. **Paid tier or session store.** This changes the go-to-market of the evidence category, and it should
   be decided before the rename (01 §8).
9. **Round 2 evidence.** Approve a recapture with the app in front and data loaded, plus Device Overview,
   the real palette, the dock (Console and Logcat), a loaded Replay, the Flow editor, Settings → AI/MCP,
   first run, no device, an unauthorised device, two devices, the light theme, and a 1280-wide window.

## 8. The three changes that move the score most

1. **Honesty layer first, in one architectural move.** Use one derived link-and-health state, one Gate
   component on every surface, SQLite included (M2), and provenance carrying a read time, never
   latency. Never seed zeros, and surface pipeline errors (M5). The agent inherits the context. Pull

   library, turn the SQLite and pid-scoped logcat tracks on by default, and add the request-to-row
   join. Then lead the website with it, and rename before the Play listing. Effect: Differentiation
